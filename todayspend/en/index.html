FAQ

Where are my expenses stored?

Only on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere, so deleting the app also deletes your records. Make a backup file for anything you want to keep.

Can I move my records to a new phone?

Yes. On your old phone, go to Settings → Data → Create backup file and save it. On the new phone, choose Restore from backup and pick that file.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. Nothing is charged monthly.

Do I have to buy Pro again on a new phone?

Not with the same App Store (or Google) account. Go to Settings → Upgrade to Pro → Restore purchase. Purchases don’t carry over between iPhone and Android.

Are there a lot of ads?

No ads for the first 3 days after installing, and never on the amount entry screen. Pro removes ads completely.

Which currency does it use?

Your phone’s region decides it when you first install the app: dollars in the US, Canada and Australia, and so on.
